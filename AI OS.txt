# 1. WHAT IS THE VEPARI AI OS?

Vepari AI OS is **not a chatbot sitting inside the application**.

It is the intelligent operating layer of Vepari AI.

It sits above the entire business platform and allows the business owner to interact with the business through:

- conversation
- voice
- business intelligence
- proactive alerts
- recommendations
- navigation
- contextual search
- memory
- automation
- approved actions

The fundamental concept is:

```text
Traditional Business App

User
 ↓
Menu
 ↓
Feature
 ↓
Screen
 ↓
Action
```

Vepari AI changes this to:

```text
Vepari AI

Owner
 ↓
AI OS
 ↓
Understands intent
 ↓
Understands business context
 ↓
Retrieves relevant information
 ↓
Explains
 ↓
Recommends
 ↓
Acts with permission
```
