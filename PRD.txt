# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Vepari AI is an **AI Business Operating System for Indian MSMEs**.

It is not merely:

- an accounting application
- an ERP
- a CRM
- an inventory application
- a chatbot
- a reporting application

It combines these business capabilities with an AI operating layer that understands the business, remembers relevant context, identifies important changes, explains what is happening, recommends actions, and—within controlled permissions—executes approved business operations.

The core philosophy is:

> **Voice → Memory → Intelligence → Action**

The platform is designed for the full MSME spectrum:

```text
MICRO
  ↓
SMALL
  ↓
MEDIUM
  ↓
ENTERPRISE
```

The **Micro** experience has deeper specialized capabilities because very small businesses often need a simpler, more guided operating experience. However, Vepari AI is fundamentally a **full MSME platform**, not a micro-business-only product.

# VEPARI AI — MASTER DEFINITION

**Vepari AI is an Android-first, React Native-based, multilingual AI Business Operating System for India's Micro, Small, Medium and Enterprise businesses.**

Its architecture combines:

**Business Operations + Finance + Inventory + Documents + Team + Automation + AI + Voice + Memory + Intelligence + Offline Capability + Integrations**

into one system.

The defining loop is:

> **Voice → Memory → Intelligence → Action**

And the defining experience is:

> **The business owner secures the application → Vepari AI awakens → understands what happened → explains what matters → recommends what should happen next → and executes approved actions.**

**This consolidated PRD is now the master source of truth for the Vepari AI build.**
