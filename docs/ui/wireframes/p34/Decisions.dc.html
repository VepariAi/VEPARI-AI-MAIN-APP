UI-UX §144 · Prompt 34 · What fits above the fold vs. what scrolls vs. what is deferred entirely
Above fold — always visible (fits in 390 × 844 px)
Kept
AI OS state indicator + greeting
Owner needs to know the AI OS is READY before trusting the data shown. Greeting anchors the context (branch, date).
Kept
Briefing preview card
Surfaces urgent items (overdue payments, critical alerts) immediately. Single tap drills to full briefing (Prompt 35).
Kept
Today's KPIs — Revenue / Expenses / Profit
Primary decision data for any business owner. Collapsed to 3 chips (~80px). Tap drills to Finance.
Kept
Talk to Vepari — voice entry card
Core value proposition. Must be reachable with one tap from home without scrolling. Mirrors elevated Voice nav tab.
Kept
Quick Actions row (4 owner-configured)
PRD §46: owner sets the 4 most-used actions. Above fold because these are the most frequent write operations.
Below fold — visible on scroll
Scroll
Recent transactions (last 3)
Useful reference but not primary — the Transactions tab covers this fully. Placed below fold to keep home surface focused.
Scroll
Low-stock and overdue alerts list
Critical alerts already surfaced in briefing card preview. Full list below fold; tapping briefing card goes to full briefing.
Deferred — removed from home, accessible via nav or briefing
Deferred
Team activity feed
Marketing card #6. Creates noise for single-owner businesses. Accessible via More → Team. Briefing surfaces critical team events.
Deferred
AI-generated weekly insight card
Marketing card #7. Relevant but not daily-critical. Surfaced inside briefing when AI detects a pattern worth flagging.
Deferred
Promotions / referral banner
Marketing card #8. Never on the primary home surface. Business data takes priority over product marketing.

UI-UX §144 applied: Above-fold shows only data the owner acts on daily (KPIs, alerts, voice, quick writes). Everything else is one tap or one scroll away — not hidden, but not competing for attention on the primary surface.
