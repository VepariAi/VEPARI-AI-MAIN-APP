1 · Component Anatomy
The bottom navigation bar contains 5 tabs. The centre tab is the Voice FAB — a 56 dp circular button raised above the bar surface. Tapping Voice enters the AI conversation surface. All other tabs navigate between content sections.

Settings
  • Bar surface: 390 dp wide × 84 dp tall (80 dp visible + 4 dp for Voice FAB overhang). Background #FFFFFF. Top border 1 dp solid #EDE5DC. Shadow 0 −2 12 rgba(59,47,42,0.08) upward.
  • Normal tab slot: flex-1 (≈78 dp wide), 24 dp icon, 9 sp label below, 12 dp bottom padding. Touch target 44×44 dp centred on icon+label.
  • Voice FAB: 56 dp × 56 dp circle. Bottom edge sits 8 dp above bar bottom — FAB is raised 4 dp above bar surface. White outline ring: 3 dp stroke, 1 dp offset (effective: FAB occupies 62 dp diameter zone).
  • Bottom safe area: Bar sits directly above the device safe-area inset (handled by safeAreaInsets.bottom). On devices without a notch the 84 dp is the full bar height.

4 · Voice Tap → AI Conversation Transition
Tapping Voice does not navigate to a new screen in the traditional sense — the AI conversation surface rises as a modal sheet, overlaying the current content. The nav bar persists beneath it with the Voice FAB turning gold.
Step 1 · 0 ms
Voice tap
Impact Medium haptic fires. FAB scale 0.90 (Spring Snappy).
→
Step 2 · 30–150 ms
FAB transitions
FAB background: #3B2F2A → #C8A96B (Spring Gentle ~320ms). Icon stroke: white → Deep Brown. Label color: #9A8A82 → #C8A96B.
→
Step 3 · 0–320 ms
Sheet rises
AI conversation bottom sheet slides up from screen bottom. translateY: screenHeight → 0. Spring Gentle (stiffness 200, damping 28). Bar background: #FFFFFF → #3B2F2A simultaneously.
→
Step 4 · 320 ms+
AI Listening
AI Core pulse begins (AI Breath spring). Gold glow oscillates on FAB. Impact Light haptic signals listening start.
→
Dismiss
Sheet descends
Sheet translateY 0 → screenHeight (Spring Gentle). Nav bar reverts. FAB back to #3B2F2A.
Bar behavior during AI conversation
  • Bar background transitions to #3B2F2A (Deep Brown) — same as the AI conversation surface, creating a unified dark zone at the bottom.
  • All four non-Voice tab icons and labels use muted color #7A6A60 — they remain tappable to dismiss AI and jump to that section.
  • Voice FAB ring becomes 3dp #3B2F2A outline (matches bar background) — FAB still visually "floats" via shadow, ring acts as a separator.
  • Tapping any non-Voice tab while AI sheet is open: sheet dismisses (Spring Gentle) simultaneously with tab navigation.

6 · Implementation Rules
✓ Do
  • Use overflow: 'visible' on the nav bar View so FAB is not clipped
  • Add useSafeAreaInsets().bottom to bar height on notched devices
  • Use Spring Snappy (stiffness 400, damping 40) for all tab press animations
  • Keep nav labels to ≤6 characters per locale (including Hindi/Marathi)
  • Gold dot indicator on active tab — visible on both light and AI-dark bar
  • FAB always 56dp; ring always 3dp; never scale these for density
  • Fire Impact Medium haptic on Voice tap; Impact Light on dismiss
✗ Don't
  • Don't clip the FAB — never overflow: 'hidden' on the bar container
  • Don't place the nav bar inside a ScrollView — it must stay fixed
  • Don't animate tab label text — only icon and dot transition
  • Don't stack label below dot — dot is always last element in column
  • Don't use a full navigation push for Voice — it's a bottom sheet modal
  • Don't reduce FAB diameter on small screens — it's a fixed 56dp
  • Don't show more than 1 badge per tab — one tab, one count
