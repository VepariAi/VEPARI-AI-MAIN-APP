⚠ Teal accent seen in Screens.png — resolution
A cool blue-green (~#2A8C8C) appears once in Screens.png on a secondary AI action chip. Investigation: the teal is not present in any other screen, brand guide, or app-flow document. It conflicts with the warm earthy palette and adds a 7th hue with no semantic role that cannot be handled by Heritage Gold or Olive Green.

Decision: DROP the teal. Do not add it to the palette. The chip in question can be styled with --color-ai (see semantic tokens below) instead.

Rule: Every color rendered in the app must map to one of the 21 semantic tokens above. No raw hex value may appear in component code — use tokens only. The 6 brand hex values (#3B2F2A, #C8A96B, #5A6B4F, #B45B4A, #F2E8D6, #FAF7F2) are the only primitives; derived values (tints at 8–10%, darkened shades, the one AI-Indigo blended value) are pre-computed into tokens and not re-derived at runtime.
